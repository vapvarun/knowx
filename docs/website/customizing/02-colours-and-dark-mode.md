# Colours and dark mode

Go to **Appearance > Customize > Colors**.

## Theme colour and page background

**Theme Color** has eight quick picks under the colour picker, and any other colour can be chosen with the picker. Every other colour (links, buttons, hover states) is derived from it. **Page Background Color** sets the page behind the content in light mode; dark mode keeps its own page colour.

Each product can have its own accent: on **Posts > Categories**, pick Automatic, one of the swatches or your own colour, and an icon.


## Theme colour

The theme colour is used for buttons, links and highlights. Link and button text colours are chosen automatically so they stay readable. A product's accent colour wins over the theme colour on that product's pages.

## Dark mode

Under **Dark Mode** choose the default mode: light, dark or follow the visitor's device. The header toggle lets visitors change it, and their choice is remembered in their browser.
