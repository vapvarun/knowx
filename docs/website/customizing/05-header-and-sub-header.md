# Header and breadcrumbs

**Included in Free**

## Header

Go to **Appearance > Customize > Header and Breadcrumbs**.

- **Sticky header**: keep the header at the top while scrolling. On by default.
- The header colour is **Header Background** under **Colors**.

The header holds the logo (up to 48 px tall and 150 px wide) beside the site title, the primary menu, the search button, the **Products** menu and the dark mode toggle.

## Breadcrumbs

The bar under the header, shown on every page except the front page.

- **Show breadcrumbs**: show or hide the bar with the trail Home, product, section, article. On by default.
- **Breadcrumb Bar Background**: colour or image for the bar.

## Menus

Assign a menu to the **Primary** location under **Appearance > Menus**. On phones the menu opens from a button; dropdowns open with the keyboard or a tap.
