# First-Time Setup

After activating KnowX, follow these steps to get a working knowledge base site.

## 1. Install the Kirki Toolkit

KnowX's Customizer options are provided through the Kirki Toolkit plugin. When prompted after activation, install and activate **Kirki Toolkit**. Without it, the KnowX Customizer panels (General, Typography, Knowledge Base, WP Login, Site Footer) do not appear. See [Recommended Plugins](../features/recommended-plugins.md).

## 2. Review your homepage

KnowX creates a **Knowledge Base** page on activation and sets it as your front page using the "Knowledge Base Layout One" template. Open **Settings > Reading** to confirm this, or choose a different static page.

To switch the homepage layout, edit the page and change its **Template** in the Page Attributes panel to "Knowledge Base Layout Two", or create a new page with either template. See [Knowledge Base Homepage](../features/knowledge-base-homepage.md).

## 3. Create your primary menu

KnowX registers one navigation menu location: **Primary**.

1. Go to **Appearance > Menus**.
2. Create a menu and assign it to the **Primary** location.
3. Save. The menu appears in the site header.

See [Navigation and Widgets](../features/navigation-and-widgets.md).

## 4. Add your logo and site identity

1. Go to **Appearance > Customize > Site Identity** to set your site title, tagline, and logo. KnowX supports a custom logo up to 250x250 px with flexible width and height.
2. Use the Customizer's **Typography** panel to style the site title and tagline.

## 5. Configure the header

In **Appearance > Customize**, open the **General** and header-related sections to:

- Enable or disable the sticky header.
- Turn the sub-header on or off (the sub-header holds the breadcrumbs and search).
- Show or hide breadcrumbs and the search field.

See [Header and Footer](../customizing/header-footer.md).

## 6. Populate your knowledge base content

- Create categories for your articles under **Posts > Categories**.
- Write your articles as standard posts and assign them to categories.
- The knowledge base templates display your categories and their posts on the homepage.

## 7. Set up footer widgets and copyright

KnowX provides four footer widget areas (**Footer 1** to **Footer 4**) and a copyright bar you can edit in the Customizer. See [Header and Footer](../customizing/header-footer.md) and [Navigation and Widgets](../features/navigation-and-widgets.md).
